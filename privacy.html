Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the app reads the channel's videos, playlists, captions and YouTube Analytics reports, and updates video titles, descriptions, tags and playlists when the owner approves the changes.

How the data is used

Only to improve the channel's search visibility and to report results to the channel owner. The data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any other purpose.

Storage

Data is stored in a private repository accessible only to the agency managing the channel. Every change to a video is backed up so it can be undone.

Third-party services

Video text (titles, descriptions and captions) is processed by Anthropic's Claude AI to write new titles and descriptions.

Removing access

The channel owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
